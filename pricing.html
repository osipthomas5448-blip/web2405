Pricing FAQ

The questions
we get most.

If your question is not here, call (718) 555-0192 — a real person at the scheduling desk will give you a straight answer.

Is there a setup or initiation fee?

No. The audit is free, the first visit is included in your monthly rate, and we do not charge a separate setup fee. The price you see is the price you pay.

What if my home is larger than average?

Pricing reflects standard single-family residential up to roughly 3,500 sq ft. For larger homes, we adjust the visit length and product allocation rather than charging blanket markups. We will tell you the adjusted price at the audit, before you commit.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Cancel any plan with 30 days written notice. There is no exit fee, no early-termination penalty, and no automatic multi-year renewal.

What about commercial properties?

Commercial accounts are custom-quoted because compliance requirements differ by industry and operating profile. Request a proposal and we will return a written quote within two business days.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Move up or down a tier at any monthly billing date. The new rate applies to the next visit; we do not pro-rate mid-month.

What forms of payment do you accept?

Credit and debit cards, ACH bank transfer, and check. Plan customers can set up auto-pay; one-time service customers pay at the time of service.

Still deciding?

Start with the free audit. We will tell you which plan is the right fit — and which one is overkill for your situation.
